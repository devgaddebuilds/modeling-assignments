## 2. Model

- Model family: k-means clustering (scikit-learn `KMeans`, 25 restarts)
- Features used: age, visits_per_month, avg_visit_minutes, classes_per_month, months_member, monthly_extras_spend
- Left out: member_id (an identifier, not a trait)
- Scaling: each feature standardised to mean 0 and standard deviation 1, with the means and standard deviations taken from the training members only
- k chosen: 4 (elbow of the training curve)
